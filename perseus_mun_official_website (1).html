About The Conference

Where Heroic Vision Meets Global Diplomacy

Named after the mythic Greek hero renowned for courage, resourcefulness, and sharp intellect, Perseus Model United Nations is established to create an unparalleled platform for youth leadership in Guwahati, Assam.

Whether you are navigating high-stakes international crises in the UN Security Council, addressing national policy in the Lok Sabha, or recording history through the lens in the International Press Corps, Perseus MUN provides rigorous debate, expert executive boards, and unforgettable networking.
